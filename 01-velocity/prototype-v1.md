## The build

- **What I built:** Build me a The Dashboard Nobody Reads that seems to have a usability issues. For context, the analytics product with a 60% bounce rate. The data exists but nobody finds the insight. Surface the right information for a decision for individuals and teams within a bank that is trying to understand various behaviour of the clients and usage of the apps/platforms that they interact with.  Include ability for the user to be able to see the multiple tools that the dashboard provides insight on.
- **Tool used:** Replit
- **Shareable link:** https://bank-client-usage-insights-V1.replit.app

## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** A dashboard that shows the performance of different apps
- **What confused them:** What the different sections mean before clicking into them. For example, journeys and also the client task completion was not intuitive in outlining which platform or app it's referring to.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** A dashboard for a bank indicating the performance of difference journeys and apps
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** The dashboard definitely needs some improvement in being clearer on what it does.
